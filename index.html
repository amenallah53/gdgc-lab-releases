Three Core Spaces, One Platform

GDGC FST Lab organizes chapter life around real student activities: learning coursework, competing in challenges, and celebrating achievements.

Classroom Space

Your central hub for session materials, PDF guides, code exercises, and assignment submissions with real-time instructor reviews.

  • Workshop libraries & resources
  • Assignment deadline tracker
  • In-app attachment previewer

Contests & Hackathons

Participate in club coding challenges, solve real-world problems, submit repositories, and earn points on the chapter scoreboard.

  • Timed challenge statements
  • Automated & admin evaluations
  • Instant feedback & point awarding

Live 3D Leaderboard

Gamified progress tracking with an expressive 3D podium, member rankings, and dynamic competency radar charts measuring mastery.

  • Real-time ranking positions
  • Skill radar domain charts
  • Weekly pace & streak tracker
